# Product Context: MaxAdjust PWA

## Why This Project Exists
MaxAdjust needs a modern, mobile-first web presence that reflects their role as emergency responders in the insurance claim process. Property owners often discover MaxAdjust during crisis moments when their property is damaged, making mobile accessibility crucial.

## Problems Being Solved
1. **Emergency Access**: Current site not optimized for distressed users on mobile devices
2. **Speed to Contact**: Multiple clicks required to reach emergency contact
3. **Trust Building**: Need immediate credibility indicators for first-time visitors
4. **Information Overload**: Too many images and content for mobile users in crisis
5. **Offline Access**: No functionality when connectivity is poor (common in disaster areas)

## How It Should Work
### User Journey
1. User discovers site during property damage emergency
2. Immediately sees emergency contact options
3. Can call or start claim process with one tap
4. Access key information even offline
5. Install as app for ongoing claim management

### Core Features
- **Emergency CTA**: Prominent "Call Now" button always visible
- **Service Grid**: Clear icons showing what damages are covered
- **Trust Indicators**: License info, success metrics, testimonials
- **Claim Process**: Simple 4-step visualization
- **Contact Methods**: Phone, chat, and form options

## User Experience Goals
- **Immediate Action**: Emergency contact within 1 tap
- **Clear Value Prop**: "We get you 180% more" message prominent
- **Trust Building**: Professional, clean design like their business app
- **Minimal Cognitive Load**: Simple choices, clear next steps
- **Always Available**: Works offline, installable as app

## Business Goals
- Increase mobile conversion rates
- Reduce time to first contact
- Build trust through professional appearance
- Enable claim management through PWA
- Differentiate from traditional adjuster websites

## Competitive Advantage
Unlike insurance company websites that bury contact info, MaxAdjust PWA will function as an emergency response tool - fast, reliable, and action-oriented. The clean, app-like experience will stand out in an industry dominated by dated websites.
